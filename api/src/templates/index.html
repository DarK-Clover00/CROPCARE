CropCare v3.0 — Live Leaf Detection & Disease Identification Platform

CropCare is an automated precision-agriculture diagnostic platform engineered to detect and diagnose plant illnesses directly from foliar imagery and live camera feeds. Powered by real-time computer-vision leaf localization, PyTorch deep convolutional neural networks, and OpenCV multi-spectral vegetation verification, CropCare identifies crops and botanical leaf morphology, detects fungal, bacterial, and viral foliar diseases in real time, isolates necrotic lesion areas, and delivers actionable agronomic treatment protocols, registered chemical/biological active ingredients with field dosages, and instant exportable clinical PDF reports.

⚙️ Diagnostic Architecture & Capabilities

🔍

Real-Time Leaf Verification

Color-spectrum analysis in HSV and L*a*b* spaces verifies authentic plant matter and rejects non-foliar uploads with informative guidance.

🧬

Botanical & Foliar Profiling

Identifies the specific leaf specimen, compound leaf morphology, and botanical species taxonomy (e.g. Solanum tuberosum).

💊

Prescription & Field Dosages

Provides evidence-based chemical and bio-fungicide active ingredients (e.g., Chlorothalonil, Mancozeb) with targeted field spray schedules.

📑

Downloadable Clinical Reports

Instant one-click PDF generation featuring visual evidence images, differential diagnoses tables, and operator safety protocols.
